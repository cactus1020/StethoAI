Clinical Reference

Thoracic Auscultation Landmarks

Recommended anatomical chest positions for probe placement when capturing lung sounds with the digital stethoscope.

RUL

Right Upper Lobe

2nd intercostal space, right midclavicular line. Detects apical tuberculosis, aspiration pneumonia, and bronchial breath sounds.

LUL

Left Upper Lobe

2nd intercostal space, left midclavicular line. Evaluates upper bronchial clearance, wheezes, and upper lobe consolidation.

RML

Right Middle Lobe

4th intercostal space, right anterior axillary line. Common site for pediatric pneumonias and right middle lobe syndrome.

RLL / LLL

Lower Lobes & Posterior Basilar

6th-8th intercostal spaces posterior. Prime location for detecting fine inspiratory crackles in congestive heart failure and pulmonary edema.
